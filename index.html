ABOUT BINGA

The heart of Tonga country

Binga sits on the southern shore of Lake Kariba in northern Zimbabwe, home to the Tonga (BaTonga), a people of the Zambezi Valley with deep ties to the river. The area is known for hand-woven Binga baskets, drum and dance traditions, and stories of the river spirit Nyaminyami.

Come for the lake. Stay for the people.
